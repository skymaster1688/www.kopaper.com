## Materials

- 1 rectangular sheet of paper (newspaper works great)
- Flat surface

## Step-by-step Instructions

### 1. Fold in Half
Start with a rectangular paper. Fold it in half from top to bottom (short edges together).

![Step 1 — fold the rectangle in half](/images/tutorials/hat/step1.svg)

### 2. Fold Corners to Center
Fold the top two corners down to meet at the center, forming a triangle at the top.

![Step 2 — fold the top corners to the center](/images/tutorials/hat/step2.svg)

### 3. Fold Bottom Edge Up
Fold the bottom rectangle flap upward on the front side. Flip the paper over and fold the other bottom flap up as well.

![Step 3 — fold the bottom flaps up on both sides](/images/tutorials/hat/step3.svg)

### 4. Tuck the Corners
Tuck the side corners of the folded flaps inward to secure them. This creates the brim of the hat.

![Step 4 — tuck the side corners to form the brim](/images/tutorials/hat/step4.svg)

### 5. Open the Hat
Open the shape from the bottom. Press the top into a rounded crown shape. Your paper hat is ready to wear!

![Step 5 — open and round the crown](/images/tutorials/hat/step5.svg)

## Pro Tips

- Use newspaper for an authentic look
- Decorate with markers or stickers
- Adjust the size by using larger or smaller paper
