## Materials

- 1 square sheet of orange paper
- Flat surface

## Step-by-step Instructions

### 1. Fold Diagonally
Start with a square paper, colored side up. Fold in half diagonally to form a triangle.

### 2. Fold Tip Down
Take the top point of the triangle and fold it down about one-third of the way.

### 3. Fold Sides Up
Fold the left and right corners up to meet at the top center point. This creates the fox's ears.

### 4. Fold in Half
Fold the entire shape in half vertically, bringing the ears together.

### 5. Create the Head
Fold the bottom point upward at an angle to create the fox's snout. Adjust the ears to stand upright.

## Pro Tips

- Use orange paper for a realistic fox
- Draw a face on your fox when done
- Make a family of foxes in different sizes
