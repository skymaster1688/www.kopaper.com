## Materials

- 1 square sheet of paper (any size)
- Flat surface

## Step-by-step Instructions

### 1. Start with Color Side Down
Place your square paper with the colored side facing down on the table.

### 2. Fold Diagonally
Fold the paper in half diagonally to form a triangle. Unfold, then fold in half diagonally the other way. Unfold again.

### 3. Fold the Top Down
Flip the paper over. Fold the top corner down to meet the center point. Flip the paper back over.

### 4. Fold Side Corners
Take the right corner and fold it up to the top center. Repeat with the left corner.

### 5. Fold Bottom Edges
Fold the bottom point up about one-third of the way. Turn the paper over. Fold the two bottom side edges inward slightly to round the heart shape.

### 6. Shape the Top
Fold the two top points down slightly to round the top of the heart. Your origami heart is complete!

## Pro Tips

- Use red or pink paper for Valentine's Day
- Write a message inside before folding for a surprise note
- Make small hearts as gift decorations
