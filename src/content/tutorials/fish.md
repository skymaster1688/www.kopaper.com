## Materials

- 1 square sheet of paper
- Flat surface

## Step-by-step Instructions

### 1. Fold Diagonally
Start with a square paper. Fold in half diagonally to form a triangle, colored side on the outside.

![Step 1 — fold the square into a triangle](/images/tutorials/fish/step1.svg)

### 2. Fold the Tail
Take the bottom point of the triangle and fold it to one side at an angle, creating the tail of the fish.

![Step 2 — fold the bottom point out as the tail](/images/tutorials/fish/step2.svg)

### 3. Fold Back the Tail Tip
Fold the tip of the tail back slightly to create a forked tail effect.

![Step 3 — fold the tail tip for a forked tail](/images/tutorials/fish/step3.svg)

### 4. Create the Body
Fold the top edge of the triangle down about one-third of the way. Then fold the paper in half vertically to create the fish body.

![Step 4 — fold the top edge down and in half](/images/tutorials/fish/step4.svg)

### 5. Shape the Fish
Adjust the tail and body folds. Draw an eye on one side. Your origami fish is complete!

![Step 5 — shape the fish and add an eye](/images/tutorials/fish/step5.svg)

## Pro Tips

- Use orange paper for a goldfish look
- Make several fish and hang them as a mobile
- Add googly eyes for extra cuteness
