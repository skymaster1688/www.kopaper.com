## Materials

- 1 square sheet of paper (colored)
- Flat surface
- Optional: glue or tape

## Step-by-step Instructions

### 1. Fold Diagonally
Start with a square paper, colored side up. Fold in half diagonally to form a triangle.

![Step 1 — fold the square into a triangle](/images/tutorials/flower/step1.svg)

### 2. Fold Corners Up
Take the bottom left corner and fold it up to the top point. Repeat with the bottom right corner. You now have a diamond shape.

![Step 2 — fold the corners up into a diamond](/images/tutorials/flower/step2.svg)

### 3. Fold Flaps Down
Take the two flaps you just created and fold their top edges down to match the side edges. Unfold these folds.

![Step 3 — fold the flaps down, then unfold](/images/tutorials/flower/step3.svg)

### 4. Squash Fold
Open each flap and press it flat into a squash fold, creating two kite-shaped petals.

![Step 4 — squash fold into kite petals](/images/tutorials/flower/step4.svg)

### 5. Fold Petal Edges
Fold the outer edges of each kite shape inward to the center line. This refines the petal shape.

![Step 5 — fold petal edges to the center](/images/tutorials/flower/step5.svg)

### 6. Roll and Shape
Gently curl the petals around a pencil or your finger. Repeat with more flowers and arrange them together for a bouquet.

![Step 6 — roll and shape the petals](/images/tutorials/flower/step6.svg)

## Pro Tips

- Make multiple flowers in different colors for a bouquet
- Use patterned paper for unique designs
- Attach to pipe cleaners for stems
