## Materials

- 1 sheet of cardstock
- Scissors
- Glue or tape
- Markers for decorating

## Step-by-step Instructions

### 1. Cut the Template
Print the cat template on cardstock. Cut out the body, head, tail, and two ear pieces along the solid lines.

### 2. Fold the Body
Fold the body piece along the scored lines to form a triangular prism shape. This allows the cat to stand on its own. Glue the tabs to secure.

### 3. Attach the Head
Fold the head piece and glue the tabs to form a small box. Glue the head to the front top of the body.

### 4. Add the Ears
Fold the two ear pieces into triangle shapes. Glue them to the top of the head.

### 5. Attach the Tail
Fold the tail piece slightly to give it a curve. Glue one end to the back of the body. Position it so it sticks up or curls around.

### 6. Decorate Your Cat
Draw eyes, a nose, whiskers, and fur patterns. Add a collar or bow. Your paper cat is complete!

## Pro Tips

- Use orange, gray, or black paper for different cat breeds
- Add a yarn ball made from leftover paper
- Make a whole cat family in different colors
