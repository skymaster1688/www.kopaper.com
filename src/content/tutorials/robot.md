## Materials

- 2 sheets of cardstock
- Scissors
- Glue or double-sided tape
- Markers or stickers for decorating

## Step-by-step Instructions

### 1. Cut All Pieces
Print the robot template on cardstock. Cut out the head, body, two arms, two legs, and antenna pieces.

### 2. Assemble the Head
Fold the head piece along the scored lines and glue the tabs to form a cube. Leave the bottom open for attaching to the body.

### 3. Build the Body
Fold the body piece into a rectangular box. Glue the tabs inside to secure. The body should be slightly wider than the head.

### 4. Create the Arms and Legs
Fold each arm and leg piece into narrow tubes or boxes. Glue the tabs to hold their shape.

### 5. Attach Everything
Glue the head on top of the body. Attach the arms to the sides and the legs to the bottom. Add the antenna to the top of the head.

### 6. Add Details
Draw a face, buttons, circuits, and screens on your robot. Add metallic details with markers. Your paper robot is ready!

## Pro Tips

- Use metallic or silver cardstock for a robot look
- Add googly eyes for personality
- Make different robot designs by mixing colors
