## Materials

- 1 square sheet of paper (origami or printer paper cut to square)
- Flat surface

## Step-by-step Instructions

### 1. Fold Diagonally Both Ways
Start with a square paper. Fold in half diagonally, unfold, then fold in half diagonally the other way. Unfold.

![Step 1 — crease both diagonals on the square](/images/tutorials/frog/step1.svg)

### 2. Create the Triangle Base
Using the diagonal creases, collapse the paper into a waterbomb base (a triangle with four flaps).

![Step 2 — collapse into a triangle base](/images/tutorials/frog/step2.svg)

### 3. Fold the Legs
Take the two bottom flaps and fold them upward to form the front legs. Fold the tips down to create feet.

![Step 3 — fold up the front legs and feet](/images/tutorials/frog/step3.svg)

### 4. Fold Sides to Center
Fold the left and right edges of the bottom layers to the center line.

![Step 4 — fold the side edges to the center](/images/tutorials/frog/step4.svg)

### 5. Create the Jumping Mechanism
Fold the bottom point up, then fold it back down on itself. This creates a Z-fold that acts as the spring for jumping.

![Step 5 — make the jumping Z-fold](/images/tutorials/frog/step5.svg)

### 6. Form the Back Legs
Fold the remaining two flaps outward to create the back legs. Your frog is ready to jump!

![Step 6 — fold out the back legs](/images/tutorials/frog/step6.svg)

## Pro Tips

- Use thicker paper for higher jumps
- Press firmly on the back to make it jump
- Have a frog jumping contest with friends
