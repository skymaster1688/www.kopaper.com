## Materials

- 1 sheet of cardstock or thick paper
- Scissors
- Glue or tape
- Markers or crayons for decorating

## Step-by-step Instructions

### 1. Cut the Template
Print or draw the car template on cardstock. Cut along the outline, including the fold tabs.

### 2. Score the Fold Lines
Lightly score along all the fold lines using a ruler and the back of a pen. This helps create clean folds.

### 3. Fold the Body
Fold the main body section into a rectangular box shape. Apply glue to the tabs and press them inside to hold the shape.

### 4. Attach the Roof
Fold the roof section down and glue the side tabs to the body. Make sure the roof sits flat.

### 5. Add the Wheels
Cut out four wheel shapes. Attach them to the sides of the car using glue. Make sure they are evenly spaced.

### 6. Decorate
Color your car, add windows, headlights, and any details you like. Your paper car is ready to roll!

## Pro Tips

- Use cardstock for a sturdier car
- Add racing stripes with colored markers
- Make several cars for a racing game
