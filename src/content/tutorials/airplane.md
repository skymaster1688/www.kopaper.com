## Materials

- 1 sheet of paper (A4 or Letter size)
- Flat surface to work on

## Step-by-step Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Paper
![Step 1 — rectangular paper in landscape orientation](/images/tutorials/airplane/step1.svg)
Start with a rectangular piece of paper. Place it in landscape orientation (horizontal).

### 2. First Fold
![Step 2 — paper folded in half lengthwise to mark the center crease](/images/tutorials/airplane/step2.svg)
Fold the paper in half lengthwise. Crease well, then unfold. This creates a center line guide.

### 3. Fold Corners
![Step 3 — top corners folded to the center line forming a house shape](/images/tutorials/airplane/step3.svg)
Fold the top right corner down to the center line. Repeat with the top left corner. The top of your paper should now form a triangle.

### 4. Form the Nose
![Step 4 — angled edges folded in again to narrow the nose](/images/tutorials/airplane/step4.svg)
Fold the angled edges to the center line again, making a narrower shape. This forms the nose of the airplane.

### 5. Create the Wings
![Step 5 — final paper airplane with wings spread](/images/tutorials/airplane/step5.svg)
Fold the plane in half along the center crease. Then fold down the wings on both sides, leaving about 1 inch at the bottom for the fuselage.

## Pro Tips

- Use thinner paper for better flight
- Fold sharply for straighter flight
- Adjust the wing angle to change flight direction
