This started as "Gir". It ended up as a pixel papercraft design.

The pixel style trades curves for squares. That makes it the most forgiving look to rebuild with a ruler and a blade.

## Variations worth trying

Nothing is fixed until you stop generating. Worth an attempt: "Gir, shown in three different paper colourways side by side". Try "Gir, shown small and large in the same frame for contrast". Another variation: "Gir, built from kraft, white and tissue paper layers".

Handy for colour, scale and pose. Useless as something to trace.
