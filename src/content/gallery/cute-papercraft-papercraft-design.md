""Create a collection of adorable pastel pink and baby handmade jewellery designs" is about as short as a brief gets. The result is a cute papercraft design.

The cute style leans on proportion more than detail. Big head, small body, rounded everywhere. Nothing here has a sharp corner to hide behind, so the shapes have to be right.

When the idea is abstract, contrast does the work. One bold colour against a quiet background.

## Notes for the build

Score curves, do not fold them. Two light passes with a blunt tool beats one hard crease every time.

The render shows how it should read, not where to cut.

Browse [everything else](/gallery/) if this is the look you are after. If you would rather have the fold lines drawn for you, start from a [printable template](/printables/). The [origami tutorials](/origami/) cover the traditional side of paper, one fold at a time.
