Nothing was cut, scored or glued to make the picture above — it is a low poly animal papercraft design of "panther", drawn rather than built.

Low poly means the shape is described by a small number of planes, which is exactly what folded paper does naturally. If you build the paper version, give it a base: a small strip of card under the paws stops the whole figure from tipping forward.

## Building it in real paper

Faceted models only look sharp if the edges are dead straight: cut with a fresh blade, fold over a ruler, and avoid double-creasing.
