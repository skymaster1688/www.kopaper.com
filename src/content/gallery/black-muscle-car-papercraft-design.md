The prompt was "black muscle car". This is what came back.

Keep the body flat while the glue dries. A warp of a millimetre shows up badly on a vehicle.

Flat lighting, soft falloff, no reflections. Paper is easier to read that way.

It is worth seeing the idea in more than one setting. Also worth generating: "black muscle car, parked on a strip of paper road with a few paper trees behind it". One more: "black muscle car, leaning into a turn with paper motion streaks trailing behind". Another angle: "black muscle car, presented on a blueprint-style sheet with visible fold marks".

Use two paper weights. Thin sheets for distant layers, heavier card for the subject, so the focus lands where you want it.
