Type "rugged adventure explorer character" into the generator and this is what comes back: a fantasy character papercraft design — no brush, no ruler, no cutting mat.

Characters carry a lot of small parts — assemble the torso first, then attach limbs and head so the proportions stay balanced as you build. Layered scenery, a warm light source and a little glow push this one toward illustration rather than studio product shot.

## Ways to take this further

The first result is rarely the one you keep. Want a warmer version? try "rugged adventure explorer character, holding one simple paper prop that hints at the story". To push the detail further, try "rugged adventure explorer character, wearing an extra layer of folded paper armour over the base". When you are ready for a variation, try "rugged adventure explorer character, standing on a small paper stage in front of a flat painted backdrop". If you would rather see it in context, try "rugged adventure explorer character, framed as a close-up of the head and shoulders only". Paste any of them into the generator and the same idea comes back with a different mood — same subject, different light, different level of detail.

## Building it in real paper

Layer it — foreground, middle ground and background on separate sheets, stacked with foam pads so the depth reads at a glance.
