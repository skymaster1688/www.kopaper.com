"classic car" reads differently once it is folded. That is the short version of this cute vehicle papercraft design.

Keep the body panels flat while the glue dries — a warp of a millimetre shows up badly on a vehicle. Round silhouettes and a soft palette do the heavy lifting here — nothing in this design has a sharp corner to hide behind, so the shapes have to be right.

## Ways to take this further

Most people generate three or four versions before settling on one. For a moodier result, try "classic car, parked on a strip of paper road with a few paper trees behind it". Want a warmer version? try "classic car, leaning into a turn with paper motion streaks trailing behind". To push the detail further, try "classic car, presented on a blueprint-style sheet with visible fold marks". When you are ready for a variation, try "classic car, standing next to a tiny paper figure to show the scale". Paste any of them into the generator and the same idea comes back with a different mood — same subject, different light, different level of detail.

## Building it in real paper

Heavy cardstock around 200–250 gsm holds rounded shapes without sagging; score the curves lightly with the back of a blade before folding so the paper does not crack.
