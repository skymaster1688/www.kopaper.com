A 2-word prompt, "classic car", and a cute vehicle papercraft design at the other end.

Wheels are the detail people notice first. Cut them last, when the body already holds its shape.

Nothing in a cute design is complicated on its own. The difficulty is keeping every soft shape at the same level of round.

Nothing in the frame competes with the subject, which is most of the reason it works.

Small prompt edits move the result further than you would expect. If the first one felt off, "classic car, parked on a strip of paper road with a few paper trees behind it". One worth a go: "classic car, leaning into a turn with paper motion streaks trailing behind". A different direction: "classic car, presented on a blueprint-style sheet with visible fold marks". Then there is "classic car, standing next to a tiny paper figure to show the scale".

Rounded paper shapes need a little give. 180 to 220 gsm card bends cleanly, and anything heavier fights you at every curve.
