"Simple car" is about as short as a brief gets. The result is a cute vehicle papercraft design.

Wheels are the detail people notice first. Cut them last, when the body already holds its shape. Nothing in the frame competes with the subject, which is most of the reason it works.

Nothing is fixed until you stop generating. Or this: "Simple car, parked on a strip of paper road with a few paper trees behind it". Last one: "Simple car, leaning into a turn with paper motion streaks trailing behind". Also worth generating: "Simple car, presented on a blueprint-style sheet with visible fold marks". One more: "Simple car, standing next to a tiny paper figure to show the scale".

For soft forms, crease gently over a dowel instead of pressing hard. The paper keeps its curve rather than kinking.

Great for matching proportions. Not a cutting guide.
