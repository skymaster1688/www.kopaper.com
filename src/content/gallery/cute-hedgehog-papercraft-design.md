Type "cute hedgehog" and the generator reads it as a cute animal papercraft design. That is the whole setup.

If you build the paper version, give it a base. A small strip of card under the paws stops the whole figure tipping forward.

## Variations worth trying

The obvious version is not always the best one. Last one: "cute hedgehog, caught mid-leap with one paw lifted and a simple paper shadow beneath". Also worth generating: "cute hedgehog, curled up asleep in a shallow paper nest under soft morning light".

## Paper and tools

Score curves, do not fold them. Two light passes with a blunt tool beats one hard crease every time.
