The cute style works by subtraction: fewer planes, rounder edges, and colour doing most of the emotional work. Animals live or die on the silhouette — get the ears, tail and stance right first, then add surface detail only if the shape already reads.

## Building it in real paper

Heavy cardstock around 200–250 gsm holds rounded shapes without sagging; score the curves lightly with the back of a blade before folding so the paper does not crack.
