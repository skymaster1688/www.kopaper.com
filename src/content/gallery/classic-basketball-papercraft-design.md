Half the charm of a paper model is the way it catches light. That is what this pixel papercraft design of "classic basketball" is leaning on.

The pixel style trades curves for squares, which makes it the most forgiving look to rebuild with a ruler and a blade. Start with the largest pieces to set the silhouette, then fill in the smaller details last.

## Building it in real paper

Build it on a grid: print a light 5 mm grid, count squares rather than eyeballing, and keep a fresh blade in the knife so every step stays square.
