## Ways to take this further

The first result is rarely the one you keep. For a sharper take, try "Sanji, shown in three different paper colourways side by side". For a moodier result, try "Sanji, shown small and large in the same frame for contrast". Want a warmer version? try "Sanji, built from kraft, white and tissue paper layers". To push the detail further, try "Sanji, lit hard from one side so the paper shadow becomes part of the picture". Paste any of them into the generator and the same idea comes back with a different mood — same subject, different light, different level of detail.

## Building it in real paper

Rounded paper shapes need a little give: 180–220 gsm card bends cleanly, while anything heavier fights you at every curve.
