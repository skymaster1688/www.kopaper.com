The prompt was "Sanji". This is what came back.

Abstract shapes benefit from hard light. The shadow becomes part of the composition.

Look at the shadow under the base. That is what makes it sit on a table rather than float.

If you want a different mood, the prompt is the only dial worth turning. "Sanji, shown in three different paper colourways side by side". Or this: "Sanji, shown small and large in the same frame for contrast". Last one: "Sanji, built from kraft, white and tissue paper layers".

Score curves, do not fold them. Two light passes with a blunt tool beats one hard crease every time.
