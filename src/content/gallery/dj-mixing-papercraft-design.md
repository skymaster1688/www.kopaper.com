Nothing was cut or glued to make the picture below. It came from the prompt "Dj mixing".

Let the edges stay visible. On abstract work, the cut line is often the most interesting part. Nothing in the frame competes with the subject, which is most of the reason it works.

## Follow-up prompts

Two or three variations usually beat one perfect prompt. Same subject: "Dj mixing, shown in three different paper colourways side by side". Or "Dj mixing, shown small and large in the same frame for contrast".

## Building it for real

Heavy cardstock, somewhere near 200 to 250 gsm, holds rounded shapes without sagging. Score the curves lightly with the back of a blade before folding so the paper does not crack.

More in [the gallery](/gallery/). [Printable templates](/printables/) are the shortcut if you want to build today. And if folding by hand appeals more, the [origami tutorials](/origami/) go step by step.
