A 10-word prompt, "i want to creat a dangerious looking dinosor full body", and a fantasy papercraft design at the other end.

When the idea is abstract, contrast does the work. One bold colour against a quiet background.

Nothing in the frame competes with the subject, which is most of the reason it works.

One generation is rarely the end of it. Also worth generating: "i want to creat a dangerious looking dinosor full body, shown in three different paper colourways side by side". One more: "i want to creat a dangerious looking dinosor full body, shown small and large in the same frame for contrast". Another angle: "i want to creat a dangerious looking dinosor full body, built from kraft, white and tissue paper layers". If the first one felt off, "i want to creat a dangerious looking dinosor full body, lit hard from one side so the paper shadow becomes part of the picture".

The depth comes from spacing. Mount each layer a few millimetres in front of the last and let the shadows do the rest.

Worth saying plainly: this is artwork, not a printable net. No fold lines are included.
