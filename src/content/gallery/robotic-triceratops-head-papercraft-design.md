The brief behind this one was "robotic triceratops head", typed straight into the generator.

Four-legged and winged subjects look best when the legs and joints are glued firmly. Heavier cardstock helps the figure stand on its own.

Flat planes, no gradients, and a palette small enough to count on one hand. That is the entire trick. Look at the shadow under the base. That is what makes it sit on a table rather than float.

## If you want a different mood

Two or three variations usually beat one perfect prompt. Same subject: "robotic triceratops head, caught mid-leap with one paw lifted and a simple paper shadow beneath". Or "robotic triceratops head, curled up asleep in a shallow paper nest under soft morning light". Worth an attempt: "robotic triceratops head, mounted on a small paper plinth like a gallery piece". Try "robotic triceratops head, as a pair of matching figures, one slightly smaller".

## Notes for the build
