One prompt, "pixel basketball", a few seconds of waiting. That gets you here.

Let the edges stay visible. On abstract work, the cut line is often the most interesting part.

Every edge here is a right angle. The charm is in the staircase outlines and how few colours it takes to read the subject. The background stays plain, which is what keeps the silhouette readable.

The subject can stay put while everything around it changes. Another variation: "pixel basketball, shown in three different paper colourways side by side". Last one: "pixel basketball, lit hard from one side so the paper shadow becomes part of the picture".

Squared shapes tolerate copy paper surprisingly well, though 160 to 200 gsm card holds the stepped edges far better.

Worth saying plainly: this is artwork, not a printable net. No fold lines are included.
