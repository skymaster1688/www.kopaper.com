Type "car" and the generator reads it as a cute vehicle papercraft design. That is the whole setup.

Nothing in a cute design is complicated on its own. The difficulty is keeping every soft shape at the same level of round.

Panel gaps are free detail. Leave a hair of space between the body and the wheels and it reads as a real vehicle.

The subject does not have to change for the picture to. Or "car, parked on a strip of paper road with a few paper trees behind it". Worth an attempt: "car, leaning into a turn with paper motion streaks trailing behind". Try "car, presented on a blueprint-style sheet with visible fold marks". Another variation: "car, standing next to a tiny paper figure to show the scale".

## Paper and tools

Heavy cardstock, somewhere near 200 to 250 gsm, holds rounded shapes without sagging. Score the curves lightly with the back of a blade before folding so the paper does not crack.
