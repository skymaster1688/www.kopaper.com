"honda civic type r" is about as short as a brief gets. The result is a cute papercraft design.

The cute style leans on proportion more than detail. Big head, small body, rounded everywhere. Nothing here has a sharp corner to hide behind, so the shapes have to be right. The background stays plain, which is what keeps the silhouette readable.
