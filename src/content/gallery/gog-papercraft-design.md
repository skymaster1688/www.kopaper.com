A cute papercraft design of "Gog", from one line of text.

Soft edges and a pastel palette carry this one. Chubby proportions, wide-set eyes, and very little geometry to get wrong. Look at the shadow under the base. That is what makes it sit on a table rather than float.

Small prompt edits move the result further than you would expect. One worth a go: "Gog, shown in three different paper colourways side by side". A different direction: "Gog, shown small and large in the same frame for contrast". Then there is "Gog, built from kraft, white and tissue paper layers".

Rounded paper shapes need a little give. 180 to 220 gsm card bends cleanly, and anything heavier fights you at every curve.
