This started as "please make a metro station aresalna in kyiw". It ended up as a low poly papercraft design.

Low poly means the shape is described by a small number of planes. Folded paper does that naturally, which is why the style suits it.

Flat lighting, soft falloff, no reflections. Paper is easier to read that way.

## Changing the staging

The first result is usually the safe one. "please make a metro station aresalna in kyiw, shown in three different paper colourways side by side". Or this: "please make a metro station aresalna in kyiw, shown small and large in the same frame for contrast". Last one: "please make a metro station aresalna in kyiw, built from kraft, white and tissue paper layers". Also worth generating: "please make a metro station aresalna in kyiw, lit hard from one side so the paper shadow becomes part of the picture".

## If you build it

Cut every face before you fold any of them. Faceted builds go together much faster when the parts are already on the table.
