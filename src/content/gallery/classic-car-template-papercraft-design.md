Type "classic car template" and the generator reads it as a cute vehicle papercraft design. That is the whole setup.

Nothing in a cute design is complicated on its own. The difficulty is keeping every soft shape at the same level of round.

Keep the body flat while the glue dries. A warp of a millimetre shows up badly on a vehicle.

Two or three variations usually beat one perfect prompt. A different direction: "classic car template, parked on a strip of paper road with a few paper trees behind it". Then there is "classic car template, leaning into a turn with paper motion streaks trailing behind". Same subject: "classic car template, presented on a blueprint-style sheet with visible fold marks". Or "classic car template, standing next to a tiny paper figure to show the scale".

## If you build it

For soft forms, crease gently over a dowel instead of pressing hard. The paper keeps its curve rather than kinking.
