Some ideas look better in paper than on paper. Rendered as a cute vehicle papercraft design, "classic car template" finally looks like something you could pick up.

The cute style works by subtraction: fewer planes, rounder edges, and colour doing most of the emotional work. Wheels are the detail people notice first; cut them last, when the body already holds its shape.

## Ways to take this further

The obvious version is easy; the interesting one usually takes a second prompt. For a sharper take, try "classic car template, parked on a strip of paper road with a few paper trees behind it". ## Building it in real paper

For soft, rounded forms, crease gently over a dowel rather than pressing hard — the paper keeps its curve instead of kinking.
