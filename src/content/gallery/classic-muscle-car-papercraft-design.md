Half the charm of a paper model is the way it catches light. That is what this fantasy vehicle papercraft design of "classic muscle car" is leaning on.

Fantasy styling brings atmosphere: a low light source, depth layers and a hint of magic around the edges of the subject. Wheels are the detail people notice first; cut them last, when the body already holds its shape.
