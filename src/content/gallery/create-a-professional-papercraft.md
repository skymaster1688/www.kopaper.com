Type "Create a PROFESSIONAL" into the generator and this is what comes back: a fantasy papercraft design — no brush, no ruler, no cutting mat.

Fantasy styling brings atmosphere: a low light source, depth layers and a hint of magic around the edges of the subject. Start with the largest pieces to set the silhouette, then fill in the smaller details last.

## Ways to take this further

If the first take feels flat, that is normal — the follow-ups are where it gets interesting. For a moodier result, try "Create a PROFESSIONAL, shown in three different paper colourways side by side". Want a warmer version? try "Create a PROFESSIONAL, shown small and large in the same frame for contrast". To push the detail further, try "Create a PROFESSIONAL, built from kraft, white and tissue paper layers". When you are ready for a variation, try "Create a PROFESSIONAL, lit hard from one side so the paper shadow becomes part of the picture". Paste any of them into the generator and the same idea comes back with a different mood — same subject, different light, different level of detail.

## Building it in real paper

The depth comes from spacing: mount each layer a few millimetres in front of the last and let the shadows do the rest.
