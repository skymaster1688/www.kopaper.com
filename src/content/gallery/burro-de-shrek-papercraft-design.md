Paper is an odd medium for a machine to imitate, yet here it is: a low poly papercraft design built around "Burro de shrek".

Low poly means the shape is described by a small number of planes, which is exactly what folded paper does naturally. Start with the largest pieces to set the silhouette, then fill in the smaller details last.

Stiffer card is your friend here — 260 gsm or above keeps large planes from flexing once the piece is standing.
