The brief behind this one was "cute black vintage motorcycle", typed straight into the generator.

Keep the body flat while the glue dries. A warp of a millimetre shows up badly on a vehicle.

Nothing in a cute design is complicated on its own. The difficulty is keeping every soft shape at the same level of round.

## Variations worth trying

The obvious version is not always the best one. Try "cute black vintage motorcycle, parked on a strip of paper road with a few paper trees behind it". Another variation: "cute black vintage motorcycle, leaning into a turn with paper motion streaks trailing behind". "cute black vintage motorcycle, presented on a blueprint-style sheet with visible fold marks". Or this: "cute black vintage motorcycle, standing next to a tiny paper figure to show the scale".

## Notes for the build

Score curves, do not fold them. Two light passes with a blunt tool beats one hard crease every time.
