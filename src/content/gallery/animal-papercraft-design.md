This started as "Box that says frog log with a picture of and great for newt salamander's frogs toads other amphibians". It ended up as a cute animal papercraft design.

Cute is mostly subtraction. Fewer planes, rounder edges, and colour doing the emotional work.

## Prompts to try after this one

Nothing is fixed until you stop generating. If the first one felt off, "Box that says frog log with a picture of and great for newt salamander's frogs toads other amphibians, caught mid-leap with one paw lifted and a simple paper shadow beneath". One worth a go: "Box that says frog log with a picture of and great for newt salamander's frogs toads other amphibians, curled up asleep in a shallow paper nest under soft morning light". A different direction: "Box that says frog log with a picture of and great for newt salamander's frogs toads other amphibians, mounted on a small paper plinth like a gallery piece". Then there is "Box that says frog log with a picture of and great for newt salamander's frogs toads other amphibians, as a pair of matching figures, one slightly smaller".

## Practical notes

Rounded paper shapes need a little give. 180 to 220 gsm card bends cleanly, and anything heavier fights you at every curve.

For more animal designs, browse [the animal gallery](/gallery/animal/). The [printable templates](/printables/) are worth a look if you would rather fold than design.
