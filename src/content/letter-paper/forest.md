## About This Design

Find peace in the woods with this forest letter paper design. Tall trees, falling leaves, and woodland creatures create a serene, natural backdrop for your writing. The design captures the quiet magic of the forest — dappled sunlight through leaves, the rustle of branches, the sense of being surrounded by ancient, living things. It is perfect for thoughtful, introspective writing that feels like a walk in the woods.

## How to Print

Forest designs with lots of green and brown tones look best on matte, cream or off-white paper — the warm background makes the forest colors feel natural and inviting. Print on 100+ gsm paper for good color reproduction. Print at 100% scale and the highest quality setting. Let ink dry completely before writing — green and brown inks can take slightly longer to dry. For a rustic, woodland feel, print on textured paper or kraft cardstock. Avoid bright white paper — it can make the forest design look too stark.
