## About This Design

A cheerful letter paper featuring soft white clouds floating across a light blue sky. The airy, open design is perfect for lighthearted notes and creative projects.

## How to Print

1. Click the design preview to open the full-size image.
2. Use your browser's print function (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P).
3. Set paper size to Letter or A4.
4. Set margins to "None" or "Minimum".
5. Print in color for the best result.
