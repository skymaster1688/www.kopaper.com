## About This Design

Reach for the stars with this space letter paper design. A deep cosmic background dotted with stars, planets, and swirling nebula patterns creates an otherworldly backdrop for your writing. The design captures the wonder and mystery of the universe — from distant galaxies to nearby planets, every element invites you to dream big and write boldly. It is perfect for anyone who has ever looked up at the night sky and wondered what is out there.

## How to Print

Space designs with dark backgrounds use a lot of ink — make sure your printer has enough ink before printing a large batch. Print on 100+ gsm matte paper for the best results. Glossy paper can make the stars look too reflective; matte paper gives a more natural, night-sky feel. Print at 100% scale and the highest quality setting. Let ink dry completely before writing — dark backgrounds with lots of ink take longer to dry. For best results, use a laser printer if available.
