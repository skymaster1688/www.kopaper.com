## About This Design

A sleek, minimalist letter paper with subtle dotted guidelines and a small header area. The clean design works equally well for professional correspondence and personal notes.

## How to Print

1. Click the design preview to open the full-size image.
2. Use your browser's print function (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P).
3. Set paper size to Letter or A4.
4. Set margins to "None" or "Minimum".
5. Print in color or grayscale.

## Ideas for Use

- Professional correspondence
- Resume cover letters
- Personal letterhead
- Meeting notes and memos
