Every page feels like a celebration, making it perfect for letters that spread happiness and positivity.

## How to Print

Rainbow designs with bright, saturated colors need good quality paper to look their best — print on 100+ gsm matte white paper. Cheap paper can make the colors look dull and washed out. Print at 100% scale and the highest quality setting. Let ink dry completely before writing — bright colors use a lot of ink and can smudge if handled too soon. For extra vibrancy, use a laser printer if available. For kids, print on slightly heavier cardstock — it holds up better to enthusiastic writing and decorating.
