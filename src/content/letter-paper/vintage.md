## About This Design

Step back in time with this vintage letter paper design. The aged paper texture, warm sepia tones, and ornate decorative borders evoke the elegance of handwritten correspondence from a bygone era. Whether you are writing a heartfelt letter, keeping a vintage-style journal, or creating historical-themed crafts, this design adds a touch of old-world charm to every page.

## How to Print

Print on cream or off-white cardstock for the most authentic vintage look — bright white paper can make the aged texture look artificial. Use a laser printer for crisp, smudge-free text, or an inkjet printer for a slightly softer, more organic feel. Print at 100% scale and let the ink dry for 5 minutes before writing. For an extra-authentic touch, lightly tear the edges of the paper or dab them with tea or coffee to create a naturally aged look.

It also makes beautiful stationery for thank-you notes, wedding correspondence, and special occasion letters where a touch of nostalgia is appropriate.
