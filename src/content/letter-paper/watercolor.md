## About This Design

Express your creative side with this watercolor letter paper design. Soft, blended washes of color create a dreamy, artistic backdrop that feels like painting with words. The watercolor texture is designed to look hand-painted, with natural color bleeding, soft edges, and subtle paper texture showing through. Every page is unique, like a small watercolor painting waiting for your words.

## How to Print

Watercolor designs need good quality paper to look their best — print on 120+ gsm matte paper or cardstock. Cheap paper can make the watercolor washes look muddy and flat. Print at the highest quality setting and 100% scale. Let the ink dry for at least 10 minutes — watercolor designs use a lot of ink and can smudge if handled too soon. For the most authentic watercolor feel, print on textured watercolor paper if your printer can handle it.
