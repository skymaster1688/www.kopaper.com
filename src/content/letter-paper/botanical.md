## About This Design

Bring the beauty of nature indoors with this botanical letter paper design. Delicate green leaves, soft floral accents, and organic vine patterns create a fresh, calming backdrop for your writing. The design draws inspiration from vintage botanical illustrations and herbarium specimens, blending scientific precision with artistic beauty. Every page feels like a page from a naturalists journal.

## How to Print

Print on matte white paper or light cream cardstock for the best color reproduction. Botanical designs with lots of green can look dull on cheap paper — use good quality paper (100+ gsm) for vibrant, natural-looking greens. Print at 100% scale and the highest quality setting. Let ink dry completely before writing — green ink can take slightly longer to dry than other colors. For a luxurious feel, print on cotton paper or linen-textured paper.

## Perfect For

This botanical design is ideal for nature lovers, gardeners, and anyone who finds peace in the outdoors. Use it for personal letters, journaling, poetry, garden planning notes, nature observations, and thank-you notes. It also makes beautiful stationery for spring and summer correspondence, wedding thank-yous, and baby shower notes.
