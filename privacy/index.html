Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Introduction

KoPaper is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use our online tools, including our text comparison tool, AI humanizer, paper to PPT converter, and paper craft AI.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or process any of your personal data or document content. All processing happens locally in your browser.

When you use our tools:

  • Documents and text are never uploaded to our servers
  • All comparisons and conversions happen locally on your device
  • No cookies are used to track your activity
  • No analytics tools are used to monitor your usage

Third-Party Services

We use third-party CDNs to deliver our website assets:

  • Tailwind CSS (via jsdelivr.net)
  • Font Awesome (via jsdelivr.net)
  • Various JavaScript libraries (via jsdelivr.net and cdnjs.cloudflare.com)

These third-party services may collect anonymous usage data as required by their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us
